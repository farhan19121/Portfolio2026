# Portfolio Projects

Projects should be presented as business case studies.

> Problem → Analysis → Insight → Recommendation

# Project 1 — Flipkart Sales & Customer Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed a large retail sales dataset to understand customer behavior, product performance, revenue concentration, discounts, and cross-selling opportunities.

## Dataset
Retail sales and product data containing:
- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Cities
- Selling prices
- Discounts
- Procurement quantities

## Tools
- SQL
- Python
- Excel / Power BI where applicable

## Key Analysis Areas

### Customer Segmentation
Identify:
- Loyal customers
- High-value customers
- Casual customers
- Discount-sensitive customers

### Revenue Analysis
Investigate:
- Revenue contribution by customer segments
- Revenue concentration
- Average order value
- Repeat purchasing behavior

### Product Analysis
Analyze:
- Category performance
- Subcategory performance
- Brand performance
- Product-level revenue

### Cross-Selling Analysis
Investigate:
- Products frequently purchased together
- Categories with cross-selling potential
- Opportunities for recommendation systems

### Discount Analysis
Analyze:
- Impact of discounts on purchasing
- Discount-sensitive segments
- Revenue and margin implications

## Verified Analytical Findings
- Segmented approximately 3,900 customers into Loyal, Discount-Only, and Casual customer tiers
- Identified a $15.36 per transaction margin gap
- Profiled 494 high-value organic customers with approximately $82.32 average spend and 33.7 repeat purchases

IMPORTANT: Use these metrics only in their correct analytical context.

## Business Questions
- Which customer segments generate the most value?
- Which categories drive revenue?
- Which customers should receive retention campaigns?
- Which products create cross-selling opportunities?
- How do discounts influence customer behavior?
- Is revenue concentrated among a small group of customers?

## Recommended Presentation
1. Business Problem
2. Dataset Overview
3. Analytical Approach
4. Key Findings
5. Interactive Visualizations
6. Business Recommendations

## Presentation Rule
Avoid simply displaying dashboard screenshots.

Every project should answer:
- What was the problem?
- What did I analyze?
- What did I find?
- What should the business do?
